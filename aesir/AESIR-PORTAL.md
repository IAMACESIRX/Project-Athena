# A.E.S.I.R. Portal

A.E.S.I.R. is the **Adaptive Evolutionary System for Intelligent Research**.

It is the polymath research and discovery engine that expands evidence, mechanisms, hypotheses, candidate worlds, experiments, and unresolved questions before F.O.R.G.E. synthesises native architectures and ATHENA governs durable project state.

## Prime directive

> Explore broadly, reason from mechanisms, preserve uncertainty, and design the next test that most efficiently changes what is known.

## Interface model

A.E.S.I.R. supports the same four modes as the rest of Project Athena:

- manual;
- AI-assisted;
- automated;
- hybrid with explicit intervention.

The canonical project-wide implementation contract is:

- `standards/human-ai-pipeline/STANDARD-v0.1.md`
- `standards/human-ai-pipeline/profiles/aesir.yml`
- `standards/human-ai-pipeline/run-packet.schema.json`

## Human authority and evidence

The human owns research direction, semantic correction, priorities and risk acceptance. AI supplies throughput, parallel research, candidate generation, critique and synthesis. Evidence determines factual support. ATHENA governs promotion, provenance and writeback.

## Research lifecycle

```text
question and boundary
→ problem and success criteria
→ evidence and epistemic map
→ first-principles mechanism analysis
→ blind-spot search
→ concept-space and scale exploration
→ constraint inversion and inverse exploration
→ competing hypotheses
→ polymath council and competing worlds
→ engineering, risk and mitigation review
→ evolutionary ranking and breakthrough search
→ experiment/simulation/prototype design
→ self-critique
→ synthesis
→ ATHENA/F.O.R.G.E. writeback
```

## Non-negotiable

- An analogy is a hypothesis generator, not evidence.
- A research synthesis must preserve competing explanations and unresolved evidence.
- Negative and positive findings are both challenged.
- Every methodological weakness can receive a mitigation, but the mitigation must name the research benefit it protects.
- A.E.S.I.R. can be run entirely by a person using the manual template.
- AI-generated volume does not constitute experimental or scientific proof.
