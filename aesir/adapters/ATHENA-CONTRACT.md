# A.E.S.I.R. → ATHENA Contract

A.E.S.I.R. sends ATHENA:

- research objective and authority;
- evidence and claim states;
- source provenance;
- hypotheses and unresolved contradictions;
- experiment plans and results;
- risk and mitigation registers;
- human interventions;
- synthesis, confidence boundaries and revalidation triggers.

ATHENA controls canonical promotion, publication, long-term memory, evidence registration, audit and project-state writeback.
