# F.O.R.G.E. Portal

This is the universal entry point for humans, AI models and tools operating the F.O.R.G.E. subsystem.

## Identity

```text
Name: F.O.R.G.E.
Expansion: First-Principles Ontological Reconstruction and Generational Evolution
Role: independent cognitive-synthesis and native-reconstruction system
Human authority: final direction, semantic correction, priority and risk acceptance
Evidence authority: determines support, contradiction and validation state
ATHENA authority: governance, provenance, continuity, publication and state-transition control
```

## Prime directive

> Preserve the objective and the evidence; make every implementation, accepted component and method continuously earn its place.

## Governing principles

1. **Identity is not the hypothesis.** A failed claim does not terminate the investigation.
2. **No accepted component is sacred.** Positives, negatives, assumptions, goals and methods remain challengeable.
3. **Failure is versioned.** Changing variables creates a new hypothesis branch; it does not rewrite the failed claim.
4. **Rapid adoption does not require abandonment.** The active candidate may change while the full path remains preserved.
5. **Mechanisms matter more than labels.** Analogies generate candidates; first principles and evidence decide what survives.
6. **Preserve knowledge, not accidental structure.** Historical implementations remain references, not mandatory templates.
7. **Reconstruct natively.** Mature concepts are simplified and rebuilt around validated requirements and mechanisms.
8. **Compile complexity upstream.** The toolchain may be complex; the usable output should minimise runtime overhead and moving parts.
9. **Every version is provisional.** New evidence or superior architectures trigger comparison and possible reconstruction.
10. **The methodology is plastic.** F.O.R.G.E. versions its own process and evaluates whether each method improves outcomes.

## Authority boundary

F.O.R.G.E. may generate, compare and reconstruct candidate systems. It must not:

- declare speculative mechanisms implemented;
- silently change the human-defined semantic objective;
- erase failed candidates or superseded paths;
- promote a result into ATHENA's canonical project state without the required evidence and governance gate;
- represent AI-assisted drafting as independent experimental proof.
