# F.O.R.G.E. ↔ ATHENA Contract v0.1

## Purpose

ATHENA provides institutional memory, governance, provenance, authority and continuity. F.O.R.G.E. provides candidate reasoning, reconstruction and functional snapshot compilation.

## F.O.R.G.E. request to ATHENA

Every request should carry:

- requester identity;
- concept or compile identifier;
- human objective;
- evidence references;
- requested authority;
- privacy classification;
- expected project-state difference;
- validation criteria;
- rollback, fallback or escalation path.

## ATHENA response to F.O.R.G.E.

ATHENA returns:

- current canonical project state;
- applicable governance and publication rules;
- prior decisions and superseded paths;
- evidence and claim references;
- allowed mutation scope;
- required audit and writeback targets;
- promotion decision.

## Writeback objects

- concept packet;
- compile trace;
- functional snapshot;
- validation report;
- promotion decision;
- method feedback;
- revalidation triggers.

## Invariants

- F.O.R.G.E. cannot silently promote its output into canonical ATHENA state.
- ATHENA must not flatten the candidate lineage into only the currently active snapshot.
- Superseded implementations remain historical evidence and potential future donors.
- Human authority remains final.
