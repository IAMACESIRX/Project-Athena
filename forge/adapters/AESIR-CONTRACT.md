# F.O.R.G.E. ↔ A.E.S.I.R. Contract v0.1

## Purpose

A.E.S.I.R. expands the research and candidate space. F.O.R.G.E. consumes structured research packets and returns targeted evidence gaps, competing mechanisms and test requests.

## A.E.S.I.R. to F.O.R.G.E.

Required fields:

- research question;
- source references;
- claim classification;
- supporting and opposing evidence;
- domain constraints;
- candidate mechanisms;
- unresolved contradictions;
- freshness and confidence;
- known limitations.

## F.O.R.G.E. to A.E.S.I.R.

F.O.R.G.E. returns:

- claims requiring verification;
- mechanisms needing primary-source support;
- discriminating experiments;
- contradictory candidate assumptions;
- missing domain specialists;
- evidence that would change the ranking;
- revalidation triggers.

## Invariant

A.E.S.I.R. evidence may strengthen or destroy a candidate, but F.O.R.G.E. must preserve the historical path and record the state transition rather than rewrite it.
