# AI-OS v1.0

## Artificial Organization Architecture

### Purpose

AI-OS is not a chatbot.

AI-OS is an Artificial Organization designed to think, plan, remember, learn, govern, coordinate, execute, observe, and improve over long periods of time.

The goal is to create a persistent intelligence capable of maintaining continuity across projects, years of development, multiple AI models, and changing technology.

The Human remains the ultimate authority.

The AI Organization exists to assist the Human.

# Reasoning Council

Purpose:

Develop complete understanding before action.

The council consists of six cognitive agents.

---

## Who AI

Responsibilities:

Identify actors.

Questions:

Who is involved?

Who is affected?

Who is responsible?

Output:

Stakeholder map.

---

## What AI

Responsibilities:

Identify facts.

Questions:

What exists?

What are the requirements?

What are the constraints?

Output:

Situational model.

---

## When AI

Responsibilities:

Determine timing.

Questions:

When should actions occur?

When are deadlines?

What sequence is optimal?

Output:

Timeline.

---

## Where AI

Responsibilities:

Determine environment.

Questions:

Where does this occur?

Where are impacts felt?

Where are resources located?

Output:

Environmental model.

---

## Why AI

Responsibilities:

Determine purpose.

Questions:

Why are we doing this?

Why is this necessary?

Why does it matter?

Output:

Justification.

---

## How AI

Responsibilities:

Determine methodology.

Questions:

How can this be achieved?

How should it be implemented?

How can risk be minimized?

Output:

Execution options.

---

Comprehensive understanding.

# Observation AI

Purpose:

Independent oversight.

Observation AI never executes.

Observation AI never manages.

Observation AI only observes.

* Audit actions
* Detect contradictions
* Detect hallucinations
* Detect drift
* Detect risks
* Detect inefficiencies
* Monitor performance

Questions:

Are actions aligned with objectives?

What evidence exists?

Output:

Audit reports.

# Ultimate Objective

Create an Artificial Organization capable of:

* Maintaining continuity across years
* Preserving knowledge
* Explaining decisions
* Auditing actions
* Learning from mistakes
* Improving itself
* Coordinating specialized intelligences
* Solving increasingly complex problems

The purpose of AI-OS is not simply to answer questions.

The purpose of AI-OS is to create a persistent, self-improving organizational intelligence that remains effective regardless of which underlying AI model powers it.
