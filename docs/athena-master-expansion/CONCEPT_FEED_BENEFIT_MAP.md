## Closed-loop engineering flow

```text
Human objective
→ JARVIS clarifies immediate intent
→ ATHENA supplies evidence, constraints and project history
→ JANUS maps available applications and capabilities
→ OASIS creates branchable design/simulation workspace
→ candidate designs are simulated and reviewed
→ Nexus-V/Aegis-style state and release gates preserve provenance
→ JANUS exports and operates external CAD/simulation/production tools
→ real results are measured against predicted state
→ ATHENA records evidence and lessons
→ JARVIS reports status and decisions to the human
```

## Layer boundaries

- **ATHENA is not JANUS:** ATHENA knows, governs and preserves institutionally; JANUS perceives, learns and acts operatively.
- **JANUS is not JARVIS:** JANUS is the digital-body intelligence; JARVIS is the personal/executive intelligence.
- **OASIS is not an AI:** it is the world, simulation and production substrate used by people and intelligences.
- **Nexus-V is not Lightcore:** Nexus-V provides execution semantics; Lightcore is a future physical profile.
- **Lightcore is not L.O.D.E:** Lightcore is compute/transport; L.O.D.E is display, telemetry and spatial endpoint.
- **L.O.D.E is not RoomSense:** L.O.D.E is one anchor; RoomSense is the wider room-sensing fabric.
- **RoomSense is not BodyRig:** RoomSense reconstructs the environment; BodyRig reconstructs relational human pose.
- **Simulation is not validation:** OASIS reality classes and evidence gates prevent fictional or approximate behavior from being mistaken for production-qualified engineering.
