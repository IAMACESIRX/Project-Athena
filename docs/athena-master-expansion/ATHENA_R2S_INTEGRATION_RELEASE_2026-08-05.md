## Canonical decisions

1. Preserve independent sensor truth beside the combined view.
2. Use confidence by domain, pixel/voxel, object, time and calibration state.
3. Separate measured, derived, inferred, hypothesised and unknown values.
4. Treat dicyanin as a matched differential experiment with measured transmission.
5. Use 10,000 unique configurations × 10 repeats as a per-domain campaign pattern.
6. Anchor simulation-derived properties to physical measurements.
7. Make material interfaces and composites first-class.
8. Keep GitHub canonical; other workspaces are mirrors/execution surfaces.
9. Put active RF/UV/laser modes behind safety and regulatory governance.
10. Never call concept engineering drawings certified production drawings.

## Sanity result

The architecture is coherent as a modular research platform and software/data program. It is not coherent as one handheld universal camera or one universal detector. The valid form is distributed, synchronized, band-specific hardware with shared timing, data, evidence and AI infrastructure.
