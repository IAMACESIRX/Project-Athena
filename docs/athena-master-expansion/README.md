## Mandatory identity model

```text
ATHENA = independent institutional intelligence
JARVIS = independent personal/executive intelligence
JANUS  = independent operative metacognitive intelligence
OASIS  = non-AI persistent digital reality, simulation and creation substrate
```

JANUS supersedes the older interpretation of the Operative Duplex AI as an ATHENA action layer. The duplex is internal to JANUS: an Interior metacognitive face and an Exterior perception/action face. OASIS is a programmable digital substrate used by humans and independent AIs; it does not contain, own, or replace ATHENA, JARVIS, or JANUS.

Reality-to-Simulation is an evidence-producing perception and compilation track that feeds ATHENA, JANUS, OASIS, Nexus-V/Aegis, L.O.D.E and RoomSense. It is not a replacement identity or a universal detector.

## Architecture boundaries

- **ATHENA** preserves institutional memory, evidence, governance, project brains, ontology and long-horizon coordination.
- **JARVIS** handles personal/executive assistance, immediate human context, scheduling, devices and orchestration.
- **JANUS** perceives and operates applications/environments, maps unfamiliar programs, predicts and verifies state transitions, and compiles validated Skill Capsules.
- **OASIS** provides persistent digital worlds, laboratories, engineering and simulation environments, games, workspaces and production workshops.
- **R2S-MSP** captures calibrated multi-modal observations and produces confidence/provenance-bearing world models.
- **Material Behavioral Atlas / Known Behavioral Maps** organize validated behaviour within explicit conditions and validity domains.
- **Nexus-V/Aegis** provide versioned-state semantics, attestation and commit gates.
- **Lightcore** remains a future optoelectronic physical execution profile.
- **L.O.D.E TV, RoomSense and BodyRig** provide display, sensing and embodied interfaces.

## Engineering claim boundary

This pack is design authority, architecture consolidation and an implementation roadmap. It does not prove that JANUS, OASIS, R2S-MSP, the Material Behavioral Atlas, Lightcore, L.O.D.E hardware, RoomSense/BodyRig or Known Behavioral Map campaigns are complete working systems. Specifications, generated diagrams, simulation runs and static checks remain labelled until independently implemented, tested and measured.

The R2S architecture is not one universal camera. RF, optical, thermal, geometric, acoustic and THz modalities retain different resolution, penetration, safety, calibration and regulatory limits. Simulation volume does not constitute physical proof; critical values require traceable measurement and validation.

## Plan and validation boundary

The root `Plan/` corpus contains Markdown, DOCX, ZIP and text artifacts plus a mirror under `Nexus V/docs/Plan/`. The remote repository pass does not replace local binary extraction, PowerShell operation-cycle execution, sensor calibration, first-article testing, hardware linting, RTL compilation or simulation. Generated reports validate only the exact source state and toolchain they identify.

## One-line thesis

ATHENA knows and governs institutionally, JARVIS assists and orchestrates personally, JANUS perceives and acts operatively, R2S measures and compiles the physical world with explicit uncertainty, OASIS provides the persistent programmable digital world, and Nexus-V/Aegis/Lightcore/L.O.D.E/RoomSense/BodyRig provide the execution, trust, physical, display and spatial layers around them.
