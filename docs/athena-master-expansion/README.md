## Mandatory identity model

```text
ATHENA = independent institutional intelligence
JARVIS = independent personal/executive intelligence
JANUS  = independent operative metacognitive intelligence
OASIS  = non-AI persistent digital reality, simulation and creation substrate
```

JANUS supersedes the older interpretation of the Operative Duplex AI as an ATHENA action layer. The duplex is internal to JANUS: an Interior metacognitive face and an Exterior perception/action face. OASIS is a programmable digital substrate used by humans and independent AIs; it does not contain, own, or replace ATHENA, JARVIS, or JANUS.

## Architecture boundaries

- **ATHENA** preserves institutional memory, evidence, governance, project brains, ontology and long-horizon coordination.
- **JARVIS** handles personal/executive assistance, immediate human context, scheduling, devices and orchestration.
- **JANUS** perceives and operates applications/environments, maps unfamiliar programs, predicts and verifies state transitions, and compiles validated Skill Capsules.
- **OASIS** provides persistent digital worlds, laboratories, engineering and simulation environments, games, workspaces and production workshops.
- **Nexus-V/Aegis** provide versioned-state semantics, attestation and commit gates.
- **Lightcore** remains a future optoelectronic physical execution profile.
- **L.O.D.E TV, RoomSense and BodyRig** provide display, sensing and embodied interfaces.
- **Reality-to-Simulation and Known Behavioral Maps** provide evidence-linked geometry, material, condition and behaviour models.

## Engineering claim boundary

This pack is design authority, architecture consolidation and an implementation roadmap. It does not prove that JANUS, OASIS, Lightcore, L.O.D.E hardware, RoomSense/BodyRig, Reality-to-Simulation or Known Behavioral Map campaigns are complete working systems. Specifications, generated diagrams, simulations and static checks remain labelled until independently implemented, tested and measured.

## Plan and validation boundary

The root `Plan/` corpus contains Markdown, DOCX, ZIP and text artifacts plus a mirror under `Nexus V/docs/Plan/`. The remote repository pass does not replace local binary extraction, PowerShell operation-cycle execution, hardware linting, RTL compilation or simulation. Generated reports validate only the exact source state and toolchain they identify.

## One-line thesis

ATHENA knows and governs institutionally, JARVIS assists and orchestrates personally, JANUS perceives and acts operatively, OASIS provides the persistent programmable digital world, and Nexus-V/Aegis/Lightcore/L.O.D.E/RoomSense/BodyRig/Reality-to-Simulation provide the execution, trust, physical, display, spatial and reality-modelling layers around them.
