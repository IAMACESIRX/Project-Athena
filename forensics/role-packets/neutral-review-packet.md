# Neutral Review Packet

Use this packet when the project needs a calm facts-first review.

## Review Order

1. Claims in `forensics/claim-catalog.csv`.
2. Evidence links in `forensics/evidence-claim-map.csv`.
3. Known unknowns in `forensics/known-unknowns.csv`.
4. Contradictions or stale evidence in latest verification reports.
5. Relevant audit events and decisions.

## Rule

Do not collapse uncertainty just to make the story cleaner. Unknowns remain visible until evidence resolves them.
