# Group Dynamics Protocol

## Roles

- project owner,
- active implementer,
- reviewer,
- domain specialist,
- safety challenger,
- historian/archivist,
- tool/runtime observer.

## Dynamics To Track

- who requested direction,
- who changed state,
- who reviewed,
- who blocked,
- what conflicts exist,
- what knowledge is missing,
- what decision authority applies.

## Meeting Pattern

1. Establish goal.
2. Name affected layers.
3. Name roles needed.
4. Let each role state concerns.
5. Resolve conflicts.
6. Assign next action.
7. Record decision trace.

## Failure Modes

- too many roles arguing with no decision,
- agent assumes authority it does not have,
- human direction lost in generated debate,
- safety role ignored,
- no owner for next action.
