# Continuity Protocol

## Intake

When an AI session or team interaction ends:

1. Capture the request.
2. Capture the result.
3. Capture changed files.
4. Capture generated commits.
5. Capture open risks or next actions.
6. Capture any decisions and why they were made.

## Distillation

Not every token of chat should become permanent core memory.

Classify extracted context as:

- immediate handoff,
- durable fact,
- decision,
- issue,
- research note,
- audit event,
- archive-only transcript.

## Continuity Object

Every continuity record should include:

- timestamp,
- actor,
- source,
- layer,
- summary,
- evidence,
- next action.

## Exit Standard

A future agent should be able to read the latest handoff packet and know:

- where to start,
- what is true,
- what is uncertain,
- what was changed,
- and what must not be broken.
