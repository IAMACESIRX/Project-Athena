# Feedback Loop

Every meaningful pass should produce training signals.

## Signals

- What worked.
- What failed.
- What was slow.
- What was misunderstood.
- What evidence was missing.
- What tool should exist.
- What checklist needs repair.

## Storage

- Lessons: `ai-system/learning/lessons-learned.md`
- Process updates: `ai-system/learning/meta-learning.md`
- Agent scores: `ai-system/reputation/reputation-ledger.yml`
- Tool gaps: `ai-system/work/backlog.yml`
- Risk patterns: `ai-system/diagnostics/risk-register.md`
