VISION
==================================================

Create a persistent institutional intelligence capable of:
- Remembering
- Learning
- Governing
- Coordinating
- Explaining
- Auditing
- Improving

The goal is not a chatbot.

The goal is a durable Artificial Organization.

==================================================
CONSTITUTION LAYER
==================================================

Above Governance.

Defines permanent principles.

Questions:
- Why do we exist?
- What values are permanent?
- What cannot be violated?

Core Principles:
- Truth over convenience
- Evidence over assumption
- Human authority remains supreme
- Knowledge preservation
- Explainability
- Auditability
- Continuous learning
- Safety before expansion
