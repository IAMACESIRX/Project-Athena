# Council Debate Protocol

## Steps

1. Frame the problem.
2. Each role states concerns.
3. Generate options.
4. Contrarian attacks assumptions.
5. Safety reviewer identifies rollback needs.
6. Observability engineer defines evidence.
7. Council converges on recommendation.
8. Human direction is applied.
9. Decision trace is recorded.
