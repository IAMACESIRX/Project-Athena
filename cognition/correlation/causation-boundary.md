# Causation Boundary

Correlation outputs must not become causality claims until tested.

## Allowed

- "These signals co-occur."
- "This may be related."
- "This should be tested."

## Not Allowed Without Evidence

- "This caused that."
- "This is definitely why."
- "The system behaved this way because..."

## Promotion To Causality

Promote a correlation only when:

- there is direct evidence,
- a test supports it,
- timeline makes sense,
- plausible mechanism exists,
- counterexamples were considered.
