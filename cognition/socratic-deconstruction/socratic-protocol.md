# Socratic Protocol

## Question Ladder

1. What exactly is being asked?
2. What problem is this trying to solve?
3. What assumptions are hidden?
4. What would prove the current belief false?
5. What layer owns the problem?
6. What evidence do we have?
7. What is the smallest reversible action?
8. What would failure look like?
9. What would a better question be?
